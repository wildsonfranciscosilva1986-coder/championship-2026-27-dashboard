Methods & limits

Cutoff and final scores

Cutoff 08/11/2026 06:45 BRT. All 180 matches have ESPN full-time status and integer scores; no live or inferred results are included.

Reconciliation

Table recomputed from 180 ESPN final scores, then Southampton’s official −4 applied. P, W–D–L, GF, GA, GD and points match the current official EFL table for all 24 clubs. ESPN live standings may lag.

Matchup estimate

Expected goals blend the selected home side’s home scoring/concession rates, selected away side’s away rates and league venue average with equal weight. Independent Poisson score probabilities (0–10 goals, normalized) produce 1–X–2. Descriptive, small-sample estimate—not a prediction guarantee.

Fixture coverage and limitations

ESPN fixture coverage extends to 2027-05-01; not a full-season calendar, and dates and times can change. EFL live table is checked at this cutoff; match-level score cards link to ESPN.

Top 5 denominators

Overall metrics use 180 matches; home/away cuts use the relevant venue appearances only. BTTS and Over 2.5 show event count / eligible matches and rate. Defense ranks the lowest goals-against average per match; total goals against and eligible matches are shown.
