Methods & limits

Cutoff and final scores

Frozen at 2026-10-20 13:38:26 BRT (UTC−03:00). All 131 listed played matches through 17 October have final/FT status and integer scores; no live or inferred results are included. At capture, the next listed fixture was Wolverhampton Wanderers v Portsmouth at 18:45 UTC (15:45 BRT) on 20 October and remained scheduled.

Reconciliation

Table recomputed from the 131 scores, then Southampton’s official −4 applied. Played, points, W–D–L, GF, GA and GD match the official EFL table and ESPN standings for every team. League order is the official EFL order.

Matchup estimate

Expected goals blend the selected home side’s home scoring/concession rates, selected away side’s away rates and league venue average with equal weight. Independent Poisson score probabilities (0–10 goals, normalized) produce 1–X–2. Descriptive, small-sample estimate—not a prediction guarantee.

Fixture coverage and limitations

The displayed upcoming schedule is bounded to 20–31 October 2026 (through 1 November coverage horizon) from ESPN’s posted 2026/27 schedule; it is not a full-season schedule and dates/times can change. The EFL live competition page served stale historical table rows, so the official EFL dated table article—not that broken live table—was used. Match-level score cards currently link to ESPN.

Top 5 denominators

Overall metrics use 131 matches; home/away cuts use the relevant venue appearances only. BTTS and Over 2.5 show event count / eligible matches and rate. Defense ranks the lowest goals-against average per match; total goals against and eligible matches are shown.
